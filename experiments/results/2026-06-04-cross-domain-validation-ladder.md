# Cross-Domain Instrument-Validation Ladder

The program is to assess a generic latent-structure instrument systematically
across ocean, EEG, social/organizational, infrastructure, and later domains.
The objective is not to produce a sequence of domain demonstrations or
prediction claims.

Operationally, the instrument is a stack rather than one statistic:

- a canonical or explicitly constructed multichannel surface;
- a frozen spectral observer that turns each channel into anomaly-score
  sequences;
- a stigmergic operator that accumulates distributed emissions;
- relationship diagnostics that distinguish coactivation, exclusion,
  succession, phase offset, and later mechanisms; and
- controls that preserve marginal/local behavior while breaking the
  relationship under test.

Each domain should pass through the same layers:

1. **Information sufficiency**
   - Establish whether the representation contains enough observations,
     variation, and repeated organization to support the requested statistics.
   - Report insufficient bins, channels, anchors, null diversity, or event
     support as results rather than forcing an estimate.
2. **Known-answer validation**
   - Confirm that the instrument recovers synthetic structures it should
     recognize and does not invent structure in independent noise.
3. **Canonical or constructed surface**
   - Measure coactivation, exclusion, succession, phase offset, entropy,
     relation change, and other generic structure before spectral observation.
4. **Frozen spectral-observer scores**
   - Apply the same diagnostics to observer score matrices.
   - Classify mechanisms as survived, appeared, or lost across the
     transformation.
5. **Stigmergic classification and connection layer**
   - Assess whether distributed emissions classify, connect, or amplify
     structure that remains below individual-channel visibility.
6. **Within-layer nulls**
   - Preserve the important marginal and local structure of the domain while
     breaking the relationship being tested.
7. **Second-level controls**
   - Compare candidate representations, orderings, or transformations against
     repeated controls of the same data with frozen feature selection.
8. **Post-hoc attribution**
   - Only after the label-free result is frozen, compare surfaced structure
     with domain labels, known events, or published indicators.

For every result, distinguish:

- statistical organization from usefulness;
- effect size from p-value;
- empirical p-resolution from a precise probability;
- representation effects from physical or organizational effects;
- transformation loss from absence of source structure;
- latent-structure discovery from prediction.
