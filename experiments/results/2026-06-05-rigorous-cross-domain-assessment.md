# Rigorous Cross-Domain Assessment

Date: 2026-06-05

## Current Method Boundary

The system is best understood as a stack of distinct claims:

1. **Observer signal:** unusual residual activity exists.
2. **Aggregate coordination:** multiple components are unusual together more
   often than an appropriate null produces.
3. **Relationship identity:** a specific pair, subset, order, or relationship
   mechanism carries the structure.
4. **Replication:** that relationship survives a genuinely independent
   context, entity, or time period.
5. **Predictive information:** past-only surfaced structure improves a
   held-out predictor under a timing-aware null.
6. **Meaning:** post-hoc domain work identifies what the structure represents.

Success at one layer does not imply success at the next.

## Most Important Findings

### Aggregate Stigmergy Does Not Establish Exact Relationship Identity

The ETT experiment makes the limitation concrete. The frozen aggregate signal
transfers, but no exact three-channel relationship replicates. Aggregate
stigmergy detects simultaneous distributed activity by collapsing component
identity into an emission. It cannot identify a stable exact relation unless a
relationship-aware layer is added and separately controlled. The failed exact-
triplet test does not rule out coarser stable roles, motifs, or interchangeable
relationship classes.

### Explicit Relationships Can Survive Stronger Controls

The newer CDIP exact-context dyadic instrument preserves pair identity and
tests it after removing exact acquisition-context and additive buoy effects.
Its aggregate pair-identity result survives a dependency-preserving node-label
null. It still lacks individual-pair and both-half replication, so the result
is bounded rather than promoted.

### Prediction Remains Unestablished

The CHB-MIT predictor initially produced attractive model rankings. A
fixed-prediction within-file temporal-shift audit showed that no absolute model
or latent-feature contribution survives correction. The surfaced EEG
structures may be real; this experiment does not show that they encode
time-local precursor information.

### Negative Results Are Instrument Results

The current workflow rejects saturated surfaces, catches retuning mistakes,
corrects across tested mechanisms/relationships, and preserves failures. That
supports the validation process. It does not by itself establish that the
discovery system is effective enough for unknown-unknown discovery.

## Next Work

1. Add generic relationship-identity, structural-equivalence, and
   relationship-change representations before aggregation: pair/subset
   persistence, role and motif persistence, topology change, lead-lag, phase
   mismatch, exclusion, succession, and higher-order motif change.
2. Require relationship-specific nulls and exact identity replication before
   calling a surfaced aggregate latent structure.
3. Run the explicit relationship layer on an externally disjoint CDIP
   acquisition/date collection.
4. Expand the organizational order-sensitive result to additional datasets and
   freeze mechanism selection before cross-corpus validation.
5. Do not pursue predictive claims until a feature family survives a
   timing-aware predictor null on a larger held-out cohort.
